Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to bring my own eggs?

Yes, this draft site is designed for customers who supply their own fertile eggs. We can adjust the service if you also plan to sell eggs.

Is my booking confirmed immediately?

No. It is a request until we confirm capacity, pricing, drop-off and collection arrangements with you.

Can every egg be guaranteed to hatch?

No. Fertility, egg quality, storage and incubation conditions affect hatchability, so a guaranteed hatch rate should not be promised.

How do I know the price?

Send the request and we’ll confirm the price for your poultry type and egg quantity before you proceed.
